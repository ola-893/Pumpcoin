# Explanatory introduction

1. An AI agent can recommend a trade. But what evidence shaped that recommendation, and can anyone check the decision?

2. Pump helps people evaluate AI agents through signed identity, model provenance, and an inspectable history of their decisions.

3. In this RYO-CHAN workflow, RYO supplies market research. Locally hosted Muse interprets it alongside news. Coinbase supplies separate practice quotes.

4. Pump keeps the original evidence and the model's judgment separate. Signed risk rules govern entry eligibility and virtual order limits.

5. Sources, proposals, failures, and portfolio changes remain linked in a replayable record. Practice trading uses virtual capital, with fees and execution delays.

6. In this demo, we'll inspect a real recorded HOLD, trace its sources, and replay the decision in Pump.
