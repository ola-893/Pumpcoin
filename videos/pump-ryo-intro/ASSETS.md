# Asset sources

Pump icon copied verbatim from app/public/pumpcoin-icon.svg. Brand wordmark uses the app's Newsreader 600 font and color. Plus Jakarta Sans and Newsreader downloaded from Google Fonts; their project licenses are SIL Open Font License. GSAP 3.15.0 is pinned and stored locally from jsDelivr for reproducibility.

Transition sounds are the HyperFrames media-use skill's bundled whoosh-short.mp3, whoosh-cinematic.mp3 and impact-bass-1.mp3, distributed under the Pixabay Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). No paid media or third-party music track used. SFX measured, not auditioned. No narration requested.

Brand-close choreography adapted from the installed logo-brand-close registry primitive: letter cascade, accent period landing, tagline and final still hold. Static DOM preserves all editability; timing reduced for a 5-second prefix handoff.

The explanatory revision adds six local Kokoro-82M af_heart narration segments, explicitly approved by the user. Audio script is NARRATION.md; exact text and measured durations are in voice-metadata.json. Dependencies: kokoro-onnx0.4.7 and soundfile0.14.0 in an isolated local venv. No credentials or paid speech service used. Final audio is normalized to approximately -16LUFS with true peak below -1dBTP.
